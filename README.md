# DIVIORA Music

An audio and video player for **Android, Windows, macOS and Linux**, with **AutoPlay Routines**: choose any songs,
videos, playlists or streams and the dates and times they should play by themselves.

## ⬇️ Download
**https://github.com/MS2022Singh/Diviora/releases/latest**

| Device | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| Android phone / tablet (Android 8+) | `DIVIORA-Music-Android.apk` | [Android steps](docs/INSTALL.md#android-phone-or-tablet) |
| Windows 10 / 11 | `DIVIORA-Music-Windows.msi` | [Windows steps](docs/INSTALL.md#windows-10--11) |
| macOS | `DIVIORA-Music-macOS.dmg` | [Mac steps](docs/INSTALL.md#macos) |

The installers are rebuilt automatically every time the project is updated.

## What it does
- **Your music and videos:** finds the media on your phone automatically, or add files, folders and links.
- **Links:** YouTube, Spotify and SoundCloud play inside DIVIORA with their official players. Direct MP3/AAC/MP4/WebM links, HLS/DASH
  streams and internet radio play in DIVIORA's own player.
- **Player:** background playback, lock-screen and headset controls, queue, shuffle/repeat, speed, sleep timer, ±10 s,
  favourites, playlists, video with picture-in-picture.
- **AutoPlay Routines:** pick what plays and **your own times**. Repeat once, daily, on weekdays, on chosen days or monthly,
  with as many times per routine as you like, plus volume, fade-in, stop-after and history.
- **Online content from the developer:** the developer publishes audio/video on a server, and every user sees it under
  **Library → Online**. Only the developer can manage sources.
